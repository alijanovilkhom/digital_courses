Digital курсы: about the school

Digital курсы is an online school for digital professions. The catalog has 12 courses: web development, UX/UI design, SMM, data analysis with Python, graphic design, software testing, mobile development with Flutter, digital project management, SEO and web analytics, video editing and motion design, cybersecurity fundamentals, and e-commerce and marketplaces.

Every course has its own mentor, a practicing specialist. Groups are small, up to 15 students, so the mentor can review each student's work.

How learning works
Classes are held online in Zoom, 19:00–21:00 Tashkent time. Courses last from 2 to 5 months. Classes are taught in Russian or Uzbek, and each group's language is listed in the schedule. Class recordings stay available for 12 months.

What a student gets
A final project for the portfolio, mentor feedback on every homework assignment, and a school certificate for at least 75% attendance and a defended project. The school certificate is not a state-issued document.

Prices and payment
Course prices range from 1,500,000 to 3,200,000 UZS. Paying 7 days before the start gives a 5% discount. Interest-free installments over 3 or 6 months are available.
